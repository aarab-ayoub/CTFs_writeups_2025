KeyForge - Reverse Engineering Challenge

## What We Know:
1. **Prompts for license key** via stdin (reads up to 4096 bytes)
2. **Validation happens** - all tested inputs return "Invalid format"
3. **Success message exists**: "License valid!" at offset 0x157049
4. **Flag prefix found**: "VoidBox{" at offset 0x15702c (incomplete/null-terminated)
5. **Binary reads itself**: Opens /proc/self/exe and processes data

- Hex strings, numeric strings  
- UUID format: XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXX
- Dashes, alphanumeric combinations
